Get Started in 3 Steps

Using iOS shortcuts is easier than you think. Here's how.

1

Find a Shortcut

Browse our collection by category or search for what you need. Every shortcut includes a description and compatibility info.

2

Tap to Add

Click the iCloud download link on your iPhone. It opens directly in the Shortcuts app — tap "Add Shortcut" and you're done.

3

Run & Customize

Tap the shortcut to run it. Want changes? Our guides show you how to customize any shortcut to fit your exact workflow.
