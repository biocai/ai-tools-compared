Deep Dive: Semrush

Overview

Semrush is the most comprehensive SEO platform on the market. Founded in 2008, it has grown from a keyword research tool into a full-suite digital marketing platform with over 55 tools covering SEO, content marketing, PPC, social media, and competitive intelligence. With more than 10 million users worldwide, Semrush is the default choice for most SEO professionals and marketing agencies.

For content optimization specifically, Semrush offers the SEO Writing Assistant, a content audit tool, and a topic research module. These tools are solid but not as deep as what you'll find in Surfer or Frase. The SEO Writing Assistant provides content scoring and readability analysis, but it lacks the granular NLP-driven term frequency recommendations that make Surfer's Content Editor so effective.

Where Semrush excels is in breadth. If you need keyword research with over 25 billion keywords in the database, competitive analysis that reveals competitors' strategies, site audits that catch technical issues, position tracking that monitors hundreds of thousands of keywords, and a content toolkit — all in one platform — Semrush delivers. In 2026, Semrush has also added AI-powered features including ContentShake AI for generating content ideas and a repurposing tool that adapts existing content for different platforms.

Key Features

  • SEO Writing Assistant: Content optimization tool that provides a real-time SEO score as you write, with suggestions for target keywords, readability, tone of voice, and originality. Available as a browser extension, Google Docs add-on, WordPress plugin, and web editor. Integrates directly into existing writing workflows without requiring a separate platform.
  • Keyword Magic Tool: One of the most powerful keyword research tools available, with access to over 25 billion keywords across 130+ databases. Provides search volume, keyword difficulty, trend data, SERP features, and thousands of related keyword suggestions for any seed keyword. Essential for content teams planning their editorial calendar.
  • Topic Research: Generates content ideas based on your niche, showing trending topics, related questions, and popular headlines. Helps identify content gaps and opportunities based on what's performing well in your space. Excellent for brainstorming content angles.
  • Content Audit: Analyzes your existing content library and identifies pages that need updating, are underperforming, or have optimization opportunities. Prioritizes actions by potential impact and estimates the effort required for each update.
  • Competitive Analysis: Comprehensive toolkit for analyzing competitors' SEO strategies — their top pages, backlink profiles, paid ads, traffic sources, and content gaps. The Traffic Analytics and Market Explorer tools are particularly powerful for understanding market dynamics.
  • Site Audit: Technical SEO crawler that identifies over 130 types of issues including crawl errors, broken links, duplicate content, page speed problems, and structured data errors. Scans up to 100,000 pages per audit on Pro+ plans and above.
  • Position Tracking: Monitors your keyword rankings across desktop, mobile, and local search on a daily basis. Tracks up to 10,000 keywords on higher plans with competitors' positions shown alongside your own.
  • ContentShake AI: AI-powered content generation tool that creates SEO-optimized articles based on Semrush's keyword data. Launched in 2025 and significantly improved through 2026. Useful for scaling content production but requires human review for quality.

Deep Dive: Frase

Overview

Frase is a content optimization and AI writing platform founded in 2018 that has carved out a unique niche by combining SERP analysis, content brief generation, AI writing, and the industry's only dual SEO + GEO scoring system. Frase's mission is to speed up the content creation workflow from research to optimized final draft.

What makes Frase stand out in 2026 is its GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) scoring — a system that evaluates how well your content is optimized not just for traditional Google search, but for AI-powered answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. This dual scoring approach is unique to Frase and increasingly relevant as AI search continues to grow. No other tool in this comparison offers dedicated GEO optimization.

Frase is used by over 30,000 content teams, freelancers, and agencies. The platform is known for its speed — Frase can generate a complete content brief with SERP headings, key topics, and competitive analysis in under 60 seconds. At a starting price of $15/month (Solo plan, annual billing), Frase is also the most affordable focused content optimization tool in this comparison.

Key Features

  • AI Content Brief Generator: Analyzes the top SERP results for your target keyword and automatically generates a comprehensive content brief including recommended headings, key topics to cover, relevant questions from People Also Ask, and competitive statistics. This is Frase's fastest and most-used feature — a complete brief in under a minute.
  • Content Editor with Dual SEO + GEO Scoring: Real-time optimization editor that scores your content on both traditional SEO metrics (keyword usage, heading structure, word count) and GEO metrics (readability for AI extraction, answer formatting, entity coverage). The dual scoring system is unique to Frase and helps content rank in both Google and AI search results.
  • AI Writer: Full-featured AI writing tool that can generate outlines, introductions, paragraphs, and complete articles. Uses your content brief as context to produce on-topic, optimized content. Supports multiple tones and styles with customizable prompts.
  • SERP Analysis: Examines top-ranking pages and extracts common patterns, frequently asked questions, and statistical data that you should include in your content. The analysis identifies what competitors are covering and what gaps exist.
  • Content Analytics: Dashboards showing content performance, optimization scores across your library, and opportunities for improvement. Helps prioritize which existing content to update for maximum ranking impact.
  • AI Answer Grader: Evaluates how your content would perform as a source for AI-generated answers. Analyzes readability, structure, and information density — factors that determine whether AI engines like ChatGPT will cite your content.
  • Templates Library: Pre-built templates for common content types including how-to guides, listicles, product reviews, comparisons, and landing pages. Each template is pre-structured for optimal optimization.

Deep Dive: Ahrefs

Overview

Ahrefs is a comprehensive SEO platform best known for having the industry's most accurate backlink database and one of the best keyword research tools available. Founded in 2011 and headquartered in Singapore, Ahrefs has built its reputation on data quality — its web crawler processes over 12 billion pages and its backlink index is widely considered the most comprehensive and freshest in the industry.

For content optimization specifically, Ahrefs added the AI Content Helper in 2025, a tool that provides content scoring, keyword suggestions, and writing recommendations. In 2026, the AI Content Helper has improved but remains less mature than Surfer's Content Editor or Frase's optimization system. Ahrefs also offers a Content Kit add-on ($99/mo) that provides more advanced content features including content gaps analysis and AI-assisted briefs.

Ahrefs' strength for content teams lies not in the content optimization itself, but in the data foundation that informs content strategy. When you use Ahrefs for keyword research, you're working with one of the most accurate and comprehensive datasets in the industry. The content gap analysis, which identifies keywords your competitors rank for that you don't, is particularly valuable for strategic content planning.

Key Features

  • Keywords Explorer: One of the best keyword research tools in the industry with accurate search volume estimates, keyword difficulty scores, click metrics (how many clicks go to organic vs. paid results), and SERP history. Provides data for Google, Bing, YouTube, Amazon, and Baidu across 170+ countries.
  • AI Content Helper: Content optimization tool launched in 2025 that provides an optimization score, keyword recommendations, heading suggestions, and readability analysis. Still maturing compared to dedicated content tools but improving with regular updates. Integrated into the Ahrefs dashboard for seamless workflow.
  • Content Gap: Identifies keywords that your competitors rank for but you don't — invaluable for content strategy. You can analyze up to 10 competitors simultaneously and filter by keyword difficulty, search volume, and other metrics to prioritize content opportunities.
  • Site Explorer: Comprehensive backlink analysis tool showing any website's backlink profile, referring domains, organic keywords, top pages, and organic traffic estimates. The backlink database is Ahrefs' crown jewel — considered the most accurate in the industry.
  • Site Audit: Technical SEO crawler that identifies health issues, performance problems, and on-page SEO issues across your site. Supports up to 100,000 pages per crawl on Standard plans and above with detailed reporting and prioritization.
  • Rank Tracker: Monitors your keyword rankings across desktop and mobile with daily updates. Shows ranking distribution, SERP features won/lost, and estimated traffic impact. Supports up to 10,000 keywords on higher plans.
  • Content Kit (Add-on, $99/mo): Enhanced content features including AI-generated briefs, content gap opportunities, and more detailed content scoring. Available as a paid add-on to any Ahrefs plan — a somewhat frustrating extra cost for users who expected content features to be included.

Semrush (7.0/10) — ContentShake AI generates serviceable first drafts leveraging Semrush's keyword data, but quality is noticeably lower than Surfer or Frase. Requires substantial editing before publishing.

Ahrefs (3.0/10) — No AI writing assistant at all. Ahrefs helps you plan and optimize content but doesn't generate it. This is a significant gap for content teams looking for an all-in-one workflow.

3. GEO / AI Search Optimization Winner: Frase

Frase (9.0/10) — The only tool in this comparison with dedicated GEO scoring and an AI Answer Grader. Frase evaluates how your content performs as a source for AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. This is a unique and increasingly valuable capability.

Surfer SEO (6.5/10) — Offers AI Visibility Tracking on Pro+ plans that monitors your presence in AI search results, but this is a reporting feature, not an optimization feature. It tells you where you stand but doesn't help you improve your GEO score directly.

Semrush (4.0/10) and Ahrefs (3.5/10) — Neither platform has dedicated GEO optimization features. Both focus entirely on traditional search optimization. As AI search grows, this is an increasingly notable gap.

4. Keyword Research Winner: Ahrefs

Ahrefs (9.5/10) and Semrush (9.5/10) — These are the two best keyword research tools on the market. Ahrefs has slightly more accurate search volume estimates and unique click metrics (organic click percentage). Semrush has a larger database (25B vs ~12B keywords) and more filtering options. For content teams, both are excellent — the choice comes down to preference.

Surfer SEO (5.5/10) — Basic keyword research that's sufficient for content-focused tasks but not competitive with dedicated keyword tools. Search volume and difficulty estimates are less reliable.

Frase (5.0/10) — Minimal keyword research. Frase provides keyword suggestions within content briefs but doesn't offer a standalone keyword research tool. You'll need to supplement with a dedicated tool.

5. Content Brief Generation Winner: Frase

Frase (9.0/10) — Generates comprehensive, SERP-backed content briefs in under 60 seconds. Each brief includes recommended headings, key topics to cover, People Also Ask questions, competitive statistics, and relevant data points. The speed and quality are unmatched.

Surfer SEO (7.5/10) — Surfer provides content guidelines through the Content Editor rather than standalone briefs. The guidance is excellent but the workflow is different — you optimize as you write rather than planning first.

Semrush (6.5/10) and Ahrefs (5.0/10) — Semrush's Topic Research generates ideas but not full briefs. Ahrefs requires the Content Kit add-on ($99/mo) for AI-generated briefs, which is frustrating at the $249/mo Standard price point.

Semrush (9.0/10) — The most comprehensive SERP analysis in the industry. Shows SERP features, competitors, related questions, keyword variations, and content patterns for any query. The data depth is exceptional.

Surfer SEO (7.5/10) — SERP Analyzer provides solid competitive data for content optimization purposes. Extracts word count, heading structure, term frequency, and other patterns from top-ranking pages.

Frase (7.0/10) — SERP analysis is integrated into the brief generation process. Provides headings, questions, and topics from top results but less detailed competitive data than the full SEO suites.

7. Backlink Analysis Winner: Ahrefs

Ahrefs (9.5/10) — The gold standard for backlink analysis. The most comprehensive and frequently updated backlink database in the industry with detailed referring domain data, anchor text analysis, and link quality metrics.

Semrush (9.0/10) — Excellent backlink analysis with the largest database by raw numbers. Slightly less accurate than Ahrefs for smaller sites but more comprehensive for large-scale competitive analysis.

Surfer SEO (0/10) and Frase (0/10) — Neither tool offers backlink analysis. This is expected for content-focused tools but means you'll need a separate tool for link building.

8. Value for Money (Content Focus) Winner: Frase

Frase (9.5/10) — The best value for pure content optimization. At $15/month (Solo) or $45/month (Basic), Frase delivers SERP analysis, content briefs, AI writing, and dual SEO + GEO scoring — capabilities that match or exceed tools costing 5-10x more.

Surfer SEO (7.5/10) — Good value for serious content optimization at $49-$99/month, but more expensive than Frase for similar capabilities. The deeper NLP recommendations justify the premium for some teams.

Semrush (5.0/10) and Ahrefs (5.0/10) — Poor value if you only need content optimization. You're paying $139-$249/month for 55+ tools when you primarily need one. The value is excellent if you use the full suite, but for content-only workflows, these are significantly overpriced.

Real-World Test Scenarios

We tested all four tools across three realistic content optimization scenarios to see how they perform in practice.

Scenario 1: Optimizing a 2,000-Word Blog Post for a Competitive Keyword

Task: Optimize an existing 2,000-word blog post targeting "best CRM software for small business" — a high-competition keyword with a KD of 75+.

Surfer SEO: Identified 23 missing terms, 5 heading structure issues, and recommended adding 400 words. The Content Editor provided exact placement suggestions for each missing term. Score improved from 42 to 78 in one editing pass. The term-level granularity made it easy to implement changes systematically.

Frase: Generated a content brief in 45 seconds showing 15 topic gaps and 8 missing FAQ questions. The SEO score improved from 38 to 72 after addressing the recommendations. The GEO score also improved from 55 to 78. Fast workflow but less specific guidance on exactly where to place each term.

Semrush: The SEO Writing Assistant provided a score of 55/100 with recommendations to add 5 target keywords, improve readability from B+ to A, and adjust tone. After following all suggestions, the score reached 72. Less granular than Surfer but the Google Docs integration made the editing process seamless.

Ahrefs: The AI Content Helper provided a basic score with 8 keyword suggestions but limited structural guidance. The score improved from 48 to 61 after following recommendations — the lowest improvement of any tool. The Content Kit add-on might have provided better results but we tested the base platform.

Winner: Surfer SEO — The most actionable, specific, and measurable optimization guidance. Frase was a close second with the advantage of GEO scoring.

Scenario 2: Creating a Content Brief for a New Topic from Scratch

Task: Create a comprehensive content brief for "how to build an email marketing strategy" — a medium-competition informational keyword.

Frase: Generated a complete brief in 38 seconds with 12 recommended headings, 28 key topics, 6 People Also Ask questions, 4 competitive statistics to include, and suggested word count range (2,500-3,500 words). Ready to hand to a writer immediately.

Surfer SEO: The SERP Analyzer provided detailed competitive data showing average word count (3,200), heading patterns (8-12 H2s), and common terms. The Content Editor then guided the writing process with real-time optimization. Effective but requires more manual setup than Frase's one-click brief generation.

Semrush: Topic Research identified 15 content ideas related to the topic, and the SEO Writing Assistant provided a basic outline with keyword suggestions. Useful for brainstorming but not as structured as Frase's brief format.

Ahrefs: Keywords Explorer provided excellent keyword data (volume, difficulty, click metrics) and Content Gap identified 12 keywords competitors rank for. But without the Content Kit add-on, no AI brief generation was available.

Winner: Frase — The fastest, most complete brief generation with the most actionable output. Surfer provided better data depth but Frase delivered the best ready-to-use brief.

Scenario 3: Content Strategy — Identifying Keyword Gaps Across 50 Existing Articles

Task: Analyze a content library of 50 existing blog posts to identify optimization opportunities and missing keyword coverage.

Semrush: The Content Audit tool analyzed all 50 articles and produced a prioritized list of optimization opportunities. It identified 18 articles needing updates, 7 with missing keywords, and 12 with content decay (previously ranking but declining). The prioritization by estimated traffic impact was exceptionally useful.

Ahrefs: Site Audit provided a health overview and Content Gap analysis identified 45 keywords that competitors rank for across the same topics. Combined with the backlink data, Ahrefs gave the most complete strategic picture of content opportunities and link building needs.

Surfer SEO: The Content Audit feature analyzed each article individually but lacked the library-wide strategic view of Semrush or Ahrefs. Each article needed to be checked separately, which was time-consuming for 50 articles. Good optimization depth per article but poor workflow for scale.

Frase: Content Analytics provided dashboard-level optimization scores across the library and identified underperforming articles. However, the strategic depth was limited compared to Semrush's Content Audit or Ahrefs' Content Gap analysis.

Winner: Semrush — The most comprehensive library-level content audit with the best prioritization framework. Ahrefs was excellent for the strategic keyword gap analysis component.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better for pure content optimization — Surfer SEO or Frase?

For pure content optimization depth, Surfer SEO offers more granular NLP-driven recommendations with term-level frequency targets and real-time scoring. Frase provides faster brief generation and has the unique dual SEO + GEO scoring system. If you need the deepest possible optimization analysis and don't mind a slightly more involved workflow, Surfer wins. If speed, affordability, and AI search readiness matter more, Frase is the better pick. Both tools produce measurable ranking improvements — the choice comes down to workflow preference and whether GEO optimization matters to your strategy.

Can I use Frase instead of Semrush to save money?

Yes, and many content teams do. Frase's Basic plan at $45/month provides content briefs, SERP analysis, AI writing, and optimization scoring — the core content features that most teams actually use. Semrush's SEO Writing Assistant (available as a $60/mo standalone or included in SEO Starter at $139/mo) provides content scoring but less depth than Frase. The key difference: Semrush includes keyword research, competitive analysis, rank tracking, site audits, and 50+ other tools. If you need those capabilities, Semrush is worth the premium. If you only need content optimization, Frase saves you $90-250/month and delivers deeper content-specific features.

Do any of these tools help optimize for AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity)?

Frase is the only tool in this comparison with dedicated GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) scoring and an AI Answer Grader. It evaluates how well your content is structured for AI citation — readability for AI extraction, answer formatting, entity coverage, and information density. Surfer SEO offers AI Visibility Tracking (Pro plan, $182/mo) that monitors your presence in AI search results, but this is a reporting feature, not an optimization feature. Semrush and Ahrefs have no dedicated GEO optimization features as of August 2026. As AI search grows, this differentiation will become increasingly important.

Is Ahrefs' AI Content Helper good enough to replace Surfer or Frase?

Not yet. Ahrefs' AI Content Helper provides basic content scoring and keyword suggestions but lacks the depth and maturity of Surfer's Content Editor or Frase's optimization system. In our testing, Ahrefs' content recommendations were the least actionable of any tool in this comparison. The Content Kit add-on ($99/mo) adds brief generation and more detailed scoring, but even then, it doesn't match the term-level precision of Surfer or the speed of Frase. Ahrefs excels at the strategic level — identifying keyword opportunities and competitive gaps — but for hands-on content optimization, dedicated content tools are significantly better.

Which tool is best for enterprise content teams?

For large content teams producing 50+ articles per month, the answer depends on your broader tech stack. If your team needs only content optimization, Surfer's Peace of Mind plan ($299/mo) offers maximum capacity with API access and priority support. If your team needs the full SEO stack integrated with content, Semrush's Advanced plan ($549/mo) provides the most comprehensive solution with the SEO Writing Assistant, Content Audit, and 55+ additional tools. For teams that want content optimization plus GEO readiness without the full SEO suite, Frase's Enterprise plan (custom pricing) with API and SSO integration offers the best balance. The most common enterprise setup we see is: Surfer or Frase for content optimization + Semrush or Ahrefs for the broader SEO suite.

How do these tools handle AI-generated content?

All four tools can optimize AI-generated content, but with different approaches. Surfer's Content Editor will score and optimize AI-written text, showing you what terms to add and what to adjust to improve the score. Frase's AI Writer generates content directly and then scores it in the Content Editor with the dual SEO + GEO system. Semrush's SEO Writing Assistant includes an originality check that can detect AI-generated content. Ahrefs doesn't have an AI writing tool or originality checker. Our recommendation: generate content with a tool like ChatGPT or Claude, then optimize it with Surfer or Frase. This workflow produces the best results — high-quality base content with data-driven optimization applied on top.
