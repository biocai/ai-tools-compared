🦄 Deep Dive: Otter.ai

Overview & Current Status

Otter.ai is the most established name in AI meeting transcription, having launched in 2017. Over nine years, Otter has evolved from a standalone transcription app into a comprehensive meeting platform with OtterPilot, an AI assistant that automatically joins your Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and in-person meetings. In 2026, Otter's biggest differentiator remains its real-time transcription — a feature no competitor has matched. The transcript appears word-by-word as participants speak, making it indispensable for accessibility needs, live captioning, and situations where you need to reference what was just said.

Otter's close relationship with Zoom has strengthened its integration with that ecosystem. The platform also offers Otter for Desktop, a standalone Mac and Windows application that can capture audio from any application on your computer — not just video conferencing tools. This makes Otter uniquely versatile for in-person meetings, phone calls, and screen-sharing sessions outside of standard meeting platforms.

Key Features

  • Real-time transcription — The only tool in this comparison that transcribes as you speak, with words appearing live on screen
  • OtterPilot — AI assistant that auto-joins scheduled meetings on Zoom, Meet, Teams, and Webex
  • Otter for Desktop — Captures audio from any application on Mac or Windows, not just conferencing tools
  • AI Meeting Notes & Summaries — Automated outlines, key topics, action items, and discussion takeaways
  • Otter Chat — Ask questions about the current or past meeting using AI-powered chat during the live call
  • Live collaborative editing — Team members can highlight, comment, and annotate transcripts in real time
  • Speaker identification — Distinguishes between up to 10 speakers in real time
  • Mobile apps — Full-featured iOS and Android apps for recording on the go
  • Otter Sales Notetaker — Automated sales meeting capture with CRM logging (Enterprise plan only)
  • AI Workflows — Automated follow-up emails and action item notifications (Business plan)

🔥 Deep Dive: Fireflies.ai

Overview & Current Status

Fireflies.ai has positioned itself as the meeting intelligence platform for teams. Where Otter focuses on transcription, Fireflies focuses on what happens after — CRM logging, conversation intelligence, collaboration, and cross-meeting analytics. In 2026, Fireflies introduced an AI credits system that governs access to advanced AI features (summaries, action items, the AskFred assistant). Each paid plan includes a shared workspace credit pool — Pro gets 20 credits, Business gets 30, Enterprise gets 50. For most teams this is sufficient, but heavy users may need extra credit packs. The AskFred AI assistant lets you query any meeting using natural language — "What did the client say about pricing?" or "What were the action items from last week's standup?" — creating a searchable knowledge base from your entire meeting history.

According to Fathom's own data, 95% of users say Fathom helps them stay fully present in meetings, and the average team member saves 6+ hours per week on follow-up work.

⚔️ Head-to-Head Comparison

1. Transcription Accuracy

Otter.ai leads with 93-95% accuracy, thanks to its nine years of model refinement and dedicated focus on English-language transcription. It handles heavy accents, technical jargon, and overlapping speech better than any competitor. Fireflies and Fathom both achieve 90-93% (up to 95% in good audio conditions with clear speakers). tl;dv trails at approximately 90%, with slightly more errors on multi-speaker calls with background noise. For English-language meetings where accuracy matters most — legal reviews, medical consultations, board meetings — Otter is the clear choice.

Winner: Otter

2. Real-time Transcript

This dimension has a single winner: Otter. It is the only tool in this comparison that shows text as people speak, creating a live transcript during the meeting. Fireflies, Fathom, and tl;dv all process audio after the meeting ends, typically delivering transcripts within 5-15 minutes. Otter's real-time capability is critical for accessibility (live captioning for hearing-impaired participants), live note-taking during presentations, and situations where you need to reference what was just said without waiting. Otter Chat also lets you query the live transcript during the meeting.

Winner: Otter

3. AI Summary Quality

Fathom produces the most consistently useful AI summaries. Its one-paragraph meeting recap, bulleted action items, and concise format are the best in this comparison for quick post-meeting review. Fathom's summaries avoid the bloat that affects other tools — you get the essentials without wading through filler. Fireflies produces comprehensive but longer summaries via its Super Summaries (available with AI credits). Otter's summaries are good but less focused. tl;dv's summaries are solid but optimized for sales calls rather than general meeting use. For daily standups, 1:1s, and general team meetings, Fathom's summary format is the most practical.

Winner: Fathom

4. CRM Integration

Fireflies.ai wins decisively with native integrations for Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, and more. It automatically logs call notes, transcripts, action items, and analysis directly into CRM records — no manual data entry required. The auto-sync works in both directions, pulling contact and deal context into the meeting notes. tl;dv offers strong CRM support on its Pro plan (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive) but without the depth of Fireflies' logging. Fathom supports Salesforce and HubSpot on its Business plan ($29/user/mo). Otter limits CRM integration to Salesforce on Enterprise only — a significant gap for sales teams on lower plans.

Winner: Fireflies

5. Sales Coaching

tl;dv is purpose-built for sales coaching and it shows. Its playbook tracking (BANT, MEDDICC, SPIN, Challenger), AI coach with personalized recommendations, objection handling detection, and multi-meeting intelligence create a comprehensive coaching stack that no competitor matches. Fireflies offers conversation intelligence with sentiment analysis and topic tracking, but it lacks structured playbook enforcement. Fathom has deal scorecards on its Business plan — useful but basic. Otter offers Live Assist (question prompts during calls) on Enterprise only, with no post-meeting coaching. For revenue teams running methodology-driven sales processes, tl;dv is the clear winner.

Winner: tl;dv

6. Free Tier Value

Fathom's free tier is the most generous in this comparison by a wide margin: unlimited recordings, unlimited transcriptions, unlimited storage, and 5 AI-generated summaries per month. There is no per-meeting time limit, no monthly minute cap, and no expiration on your meeting library. tl;dv also offers unlimited recordings with 10 AI notes per month. Fireflies offers unlimited meetings on its free plan but with limited AI credit access. Otter's free plan is the most restrictive: 300 minutes/month with a 30-minute per-conversation cap and only 3 file imports per month. For users who want a capable meeting assistant without paying, Fathom is the obvious choice.

Winner: Fathom

7. Language Support

Fireflies.ai supports 100+ languages for transcription — the broadest coverage by a massive margin. This includes Asian languages (Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, Cantonese), European languages (French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese), Middle Eastern languages (Arabic, Hebrew, Turkish), and many more. Fathom supports 38 languages. tl;dv supports 30+ languages. Otter supports only 6 languages (English, Spanish, French, Italian, German, Portuguese). For global teams with multilingual meetings, Fireflies is the only viable option — the gap between 100+ languages and 38 is significant in practice.

Winner: Fireflies

8. Mobile App

Otter and Fireflies both offer full-featured iOS and Android apps. Otter's mobile apps are particularly strong — you can record in-person meetings, access live transcription, and sync everything back to your workspace. Fireflies' mobile apps support recording and search across your meeting library. Fathom and tl;dv have no mobile apps at all — they are desktop/browser only. For professionals who attend meetings on the go, present at conferences, or need to capture in-person conversations, Otter's mobile experience is the most complete.

Winner: Otter

🧪 Real-World Test Scenarios

Scenario 1: Weekly Team Standup Meeting

Task: A 8-person product team runs daily 15-minute standups on Google Meet. They need accurate notes, action items, and a searchable record — but no fancy CRM logging or sales coaching features. Budget is tight; most team members are not power users.

Best tool: Fathom. The free tier covers unlimited recordings and 5 AI summaries/month (more than enough for daily standups). Fathom's concise summaries — one paragraph plus action items — are perfect for standups where you just need the quick recap. No bot configuration headaches, no per-meeting time limits, and no monthly minute caps. Fireflies works too but adds unnecessary complexity for a simple standup. Otter's 300-minute free cap would run out within two weeks. tl;dv is overkill with its sales coaching features.

Scenario 2: Sales Discovery Call with CRM Sync Needed

Task: A 10-person B2B sales team uses Salesforce. They need every discovery and demo call automatically transcribed, summarized, and logged to the correct opportunity record. The sales manager also wants conversation intelligence — talk-time ratios, competitor mentions, and deal risk signals.

Best tool: Fireflies. The native Salesforce integration automatically logs transcripts, summaries, action items, and topic tags to the corresponding opportunity — no manual entry. The AskFred AI lets the manager query across all calls: "Show me every call where the competitor was mentioned this quarter." Conversation intelligence tracks talk-time ratios, sentiment trends, and deal velocity. Estimated cost: ~$1,200/year (10 Pro users at $10/user/mo annual). tl;dv Business would cost $7,080/year — 6x more for similar CRM support. Otter's Salesforce integration requires Enterprise, making it impractical.

Scenario 3: Multilingual Client Consultation

Task: A consulting firm runs client meetings in English, Mandarin, and Spanish across three offices. They need accurate transcription in all three languages, searchable transcripts, and summaries that can be shared with non-attendees who may not speak the original meeting language.

Best tool: Fireflies. With 100+ language support, Fireflies is the only tool that handles all three languages reliably. Transcripts in Mandarin and Spanish are processed with the same quality as English — a claim no competitor can make. Otter supports only English, Spanish, French, Italian, German, and Portuguese (Mandarin is not supported). Fathom's 38 languages include Mandarin but with lower accuracy than Fireflies for Asian languages. The ability to search across multilingual transcripts and build a cross-language knowledge base is unique to Fireflies in this comparison.

If you want a capable meeting assistant without paying, Fathom's free tier is unmatched: unlimited recordings, unlimited transcriptions, unlimited storage, and 5 AI summaries per month. Even on paid plans, Fathom's one-paragraph summaries and action item extraction are the best in this comparison for quick post-meeting reviews. Best for Sales Coaching & Deal Intelligence -> tl;dv

For revenue teams running structured sales methodologies (BANT, MEDDICC, SPIN, Challenger), tl;dv's playbook tracking, AI coach, and multi-meeting intelligence are unmatched. The video clipping and sharing features are the best in this comparison for creating training clips from actual calls. However, the $59/user/mo Business plan is expensive — best justified for teams of 10+ where coaching ROI is measurable.

Best Overall Value -> Fathom

For most users — individuals, small teams, freelancers, and anyone who wants AI meeting notes without complexity — Fathom delivers the best overall value. The generous free tier means many users will never need to pay. For those who do upgrade, the paid plans offer good features at competitive prices. Fathom's simplicity is its superpower: install, auto-join, get your summary, move on. No credits to track, no minutes to manage, no integrations to configure unless you want them. For 80% of meeting use cases, Fathom is the right answer.

Otter's real-time capability makes it essential for accessibility (live captioning for hearing-impaired participants), live note-taking during presentations, and situations where you need to immediately reference what was just said. Otter Chat also lets you query the live transcript during the meeting.

Which AI meeting assistant is best for sales teams?

Fireflies.ai is best for sales teams due to its deep CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho) with automatic logging. tl;dv is also strong with playbook tracking (BANT, MEDDICC) and objection handling detection. The choice between them depends on your priority: choose Fireflies for CRM automation and multilingual support at a lower price ($10/user/mo Pro), or choose tl;dv for structured sales coaching and video clip creation ($18/user/mo Pro). For most sales teams, Fireflies at the Pro tier offers the best balance of CRM depth and cost.

Can I use these tools without a visible bot joining my meeting?

Fathom offers a bot-free capture option (launched in beta in April 2026 as part of Fathom 3.0). This means Fathom can record and transcribe your meeting without a visible bot participant appearing in the attendee list — a significant advantage for client-facing calls where bot presence may be unprofessional. All other tools (Otter, Fireflies, tl;dv) require a visible bot to join your meetings. Fathom's bot-free option is still in beta and may not support all conferencing platforms equally, but it represents a clear differentiator.

How accurate is AI meeting transcription?

Otter.ai has the highest transcription accuracy at 93-95%. Fireflies and Fathom both achieve 90-93% (up to 95% in good audio conditions with clear speakers and minimal background noise). tl;dv is approximately 90% accurate. All four tools handle English well but accuracy drops for accented speech, multiple speakers talking simultaneously, and poor audio quality. Technical terminology, industry jargon, and proper nouns can also reduce accuracy. For critical meetings where every word matters, Otter is the most reliable choice.

Do any AI meeting assistants work without Zoom, Teams, or Meet?

Otter offers Otter for Desktop (Mac/Windows) that captures audio from any application on your computer — not just video conferencing tools. This means you can transcribe in-person meetings, phone calls, webinars, YouTube videos, or any audio playing on your device. Fireflies supports additional platforms like Webex, BlueJeans, and Google Calendar-based meetings. Fathom and tl;dv work with Zoom, Meet, and Teams but require one of these platforms as the base. For capturing conversations outside of standard conferencing tools, Otter for Desktop is the only option in this comparison.
